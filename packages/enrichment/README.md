Section 12 step 8. Not started.

Provider adapters go behind one interface. Two constraints carry over:

- Enrichment fills `personal_reason` candidates for a human to confirm. It does
  not auto-fill the INV-3 gate. A machine-generated sentence is not a specific,
  verifiable reason for contact.
- No provider whose purpose is LinkedIn scraping or programmatic LinkedIn
  messaging (INV-8), regardless of how the provider describes itself.
